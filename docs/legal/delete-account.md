# Delete your Pushly account

## In the app (fastest)
1. Open Pushly and go to **Profile → Account & data**.
2. Tap **Delete account** and confirm. If you signed in a while ago, the app asks you to sign in again first.

Your account is deleted **immediately and permanently**.

## Without the app
Send an email to **dany.oujesky@gmail.com** from the email address of your account (or, if you used Sign in with
Apple / Google, tell us your username) with the subject **"Delete my Pushly account"**. We delete it within 30 days
and confirm by email.

## What is deleted
Your profile, sign-in, alarms synced to our servers, workout sessions and logs, statistics, streaks, achievements,
trophies, friends, kudos, clan membership, clan messages, ghost runs, device records, notification settings and
reports you sent.

## What remains
- An irreversible hash that only proves a deletion happened.
- Other players' battle history keeps a "Deleted user" entry with the score, without your name.
- Backups at our hosting provider are overwritten within 30 days.
- Records we must keep by law (for example about purchases), only as long as the law requires.

**Deleting your account does not cancel a Pushly Pro subscription.** Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play
account.

Want a copy first? In the app: **Profile → Account & data → Download my data**.
