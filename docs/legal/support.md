# Pushly support

Questions, bugs, ideas, or a problem with your account? Email **dany.oujesky@gmail.com** — please include your
username, phone model and what happened.

## Common questions

**The alarm didn't ring.** Open Pushly: the Alarm screen shows a warning if a permission or system setting can stop
alarms. Tap it to fix the setting. On Android, also allow exact alarms, full-screen notifications and turn off battery
optimisation for Pushly.

**Reps are not counted.** Put the phone on the floor 1–2 m away, side-on or facing you, so your whole body is
visible, in good light. Go all the way down and fully up.

**My session doesn't count for the leaderboard.** Our anti-cheat checks can hold back sessions that look unusual.
Write to us for a human review.

**How do I delete my account?** See [Delete your account](delete-account.html).

**Is my camera video uploaded?** No. It is analysed only on your phone. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.html).

**Reporting someone.** Use **Report** on their profile, message or battle. See the
[Community Guidelines](community.html).
