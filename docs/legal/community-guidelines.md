# Pushly — Community Guidelines (DRAFT)

Pushly is about getting stronger together. Keep it that way:

- **Do your own reps.** No fake videos, replays, other people standing in, or modified apps. Every rep is checked.
- **Respect people.** No harassment, hate, threats, sexual content or bullying — in chat, usernames, clan names or
  on camera.
- **Keep video safe.** Battles show your camera to your opponent. Wear clothes you'd train in at a gym; no nudity,
  no showing others who didn't agree.
- **No spam or selling.** No ads, links to sell things or begging in chat.
- **Protect privacy.** Don't share anyone's personal information.
- **Report, don't retaliate.** Use Report and Block — we review reports and act (warnings, removing reps, trophy
  resets, suspensions).
