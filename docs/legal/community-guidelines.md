# Pushly — Community Guidelines

**Effective date:** 21 October 2026

Pushly is about getting stronger together. These rules apply everywhere other people can see you: usernames, avatars,
clan names and descriptions, clan chat, battles and live video. They are part of the [Terms of Use](terms.html).

## Do your own reps
- Every rep must be done by you, live, in front of the camera.
- No videos, screens, recordings, mirror tricks, stand-ins, modified apps, emulators, automation or bug exploits.
- No second accounts to farm trophies, battles or clan war points.

## Respect people
- No harassment, bullying, threats, hate speech or discrimination (for example about origin, religion, gender,
  sexuality, disability or body).
- No sexual content, and nothing sexual towards anyone, ever.
- No content that glorifies violence, self-harm or dangerous challenges.
- Trash talk about push-ups is fine. Attacking the person is not.

## Keep live video safe
- Battles show your camera to your opponent. Dress as you would at a gym; no nudity or underwear-only.
- Don't show other people who didn't agree, or personal details (documents, addresses, screens).
- No offensive gestures, symbols or signs on camera.

## Protect privacy
- Don't share your or anyone else's contact details, addresses, social profiles or private information.
- Don't try to find out who someone is behind a username.

## No spam or selling
- No ads, links, promotions, begging for trophies or money, or selling accounts.

## Report, don't retaliate
- Use **Report** and **Block** — they are on every profile, chat message and battle. Blocking hides you from each
  other everywhere.
- We review reports and act in proportion: a warning, removing content, excluding sessions, correcting trophies,
  removing someone from a clan, temporary suspension, or closing the account. Serious cases (threats, sexual content
  involving minors, real danger) lead to immediate closure and, where required, a report to the authorities.
- You can ask for a human review of a decision: **dany.oujesky@gmail.com**.

## Train safely
- Stop if something hurts. Don't push others to exercise in unsafe ways.
