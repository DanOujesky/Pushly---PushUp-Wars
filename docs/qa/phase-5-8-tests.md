# Phases 5–8 — what to try on the phones (after the first green CI build)

Use two accounts (two phones, or a friend) for the social parts.

## Stats & achievements (Profile → Stats & achievements)
- [ ] After a wake challenge or a training: the result screen shows **+XP**, the level bar, unlocked achievements
      (first wake → "First Wake") and "New record" when you beat your best set.
- [ ] Stats: switch Week / Month / Year / All; tiles show numbers with ↑ / ↓ vs. the previous period.
- [ ] Daily bars: tap / drag a bar → its numbers appear. Cumulative line, wake consistency dots, best-set stars,
      heatmap look right in the dark theme.
- [ ] Achievements screen: unlocked ones coloured, locked ones dimmed with a lock.

## Friends (Profile → Friends & activity)
- [ ] Search the other account's username (2+ letters) → Add → the other phone sees the request → Accept.
- [ ] Share my link opens the share sheet with `https://pushly.app/u/<name>`.
- [ ] After a wake challenge on phone B, phone A sees it in Activity → 👊 → count goes up once (second tap does nothing).
- [ ] Block → the blocked user can't find you any more.

## Ranks tab
- [ ] Wake / Streak / Push-ups × Global / Country / Friends × periods load; your row is highlighted or pinned at the
      bottom. "Leaderboards visible" off in Profile → you show as "Anonymous athlete" to others.
      (Global boards refresh every minute on the server.)

## Battles (Battle tab)
- [ ] Phone A: Challenge a friend → Max reps 60 s. Phone B: Challenges for you → Fight.
- [ ] Both: get into the push-up position → countdown 3-2-1 → count; the opponent's number updates about once a second.
- [ ] After 60 s: "Checking the result…" → Victory / Defeat with the final counts (ranked: trophies ±).
- [ ] Leave during the lobby → battle cancelled, no trophies.
- [ ] Quick match: both phones tap the same mode within a few seconds → matched.

## Clans (Clan tab)
- [ ] Create a clan (name + tag), the other account finds it in search and joins (open) or asks (by request → accept).
- [ ] Promote / remove via long-press on a member (only the allowed actions appear).
- [ ] Clan chat: messages and 👊 🔥 💪 appear on both phones within ~5 s; swear words come back masked.
- [ ] Wars start on Mondays for clans with ≥ 5 members — check the war card the following week.

## Account & data (Profile → Account & data)
- [ ] Toggle notification categories (saved after leaving and coming back).
- [ ] Download my data → share sheet with a JSON file.
- [ ] Delete account (use a test account!) → if you signed in more than 15 minutes ago the app asks you to sign in
      again first.
